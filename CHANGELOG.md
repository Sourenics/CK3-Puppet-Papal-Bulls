# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Four new puppet actions for a Pope (the head of a faith with a papacy) who is a player's external clerical puppet: Call a Crusade, Declare a Heresy, Permit a Tenet and Forbid a Tenet, each enacted as a papal bull. They appear in your Decisions window, marked with the puppet icon.
- The Pope must meet the vanilla requirements of Enact a Bull and pays its piety cost; the cooldowns are shared with his own bulls.
- Only available when the puppeteer is a player.
- Every text reuses the game's own localization, so the mod is in every game language.
